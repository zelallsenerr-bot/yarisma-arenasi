# Revision 200

Milyonluk Resim: 500 authored visual questions across 100 subjects and 500 distinct real stock photographs. Local photo sources and license links are recorded in dist/assets/picture200/sources.json and sources.html. Seven mismatched candidate images were rejected after contact-sheet review. The 12-step ladder scales historical amounts by five for virtual game currency; first four questions have 60 seconds, remaining questions 120 seconds. Picture-first preview, withdraw, ask a relative, change question and buy question are implemented.

Purchase uncertainty: the Turkish official description (https://www.nowtv.com.tr/Milyonluk-Resim/bilgi) does not establish the exact buy-question fee. The international format description (https://tvblik.nl/bankgiro-loterij-the-big-picture) describes a 25 percent fee. This game uses that fee as an explicitly labeled adaptation, with no claim of verified Turkish broadcast equivalence.

Bir İçeri Bir Dışarı: starts at 10,000 TL; 14 boxes, alternating keep/discard, first keep. Cash cards 50,000 / 20,000 / 10,000 / 5,000 / 5,000 / 5,000 / 1,000; multipliers x5 and x2; penalties half, fifth, fifth, tenth and bankruptcy. Bankruptcy resets bank but does not end play. Zero times any multiplier remains zero and is not celebrated as a gain. Official format reference: https://www.nowtv.com.tr/Bir-Iceri-Bir-Disari/bilgi . User supplied TV screenshots determine visual layout and effects.

After every reveal the maximum possible reachable bank is calculated from the remaining effect multiset and remaining keep turns by subset dynamic programming. This is an optimistic reachable amount, not guaranteed winnings. Its counter and progress bar animate from the preceding target. Independent brute-force tests verify smaller remaining sets.
